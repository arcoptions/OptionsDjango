  'thin' is a hole in the option cache, not a verdict. Of the 11 months
  that can be scored the hurdle runs 18% to unreachable, and in 2026-03
  even a perfect read lost money.

  Splitting the same data by how the broad tape behaved makes the
  mechanism explicit:

  days when MORE scan names rose than usual     2,258 trades  break-even 48.7%
  days when FEWER rose                          1,130 trades  break-even 87.9%

  Read that as: the read has to be far better on exactly the days it is
  hardest to be right. Most of what this trade wins or loses is the
  market's direction, not the individual chart's.

  BUT THAT SPLIT IS NOT A RULE. It is cut on how the trades THEMSELVES
  turned out, so it explains the mechanism and cannot be applied in
  advance. The tradeable version is below.

CAN THE REGIME BE CALLED IN ADVANCE? (breadth at the prior close)
============================================================================================================

  prior breadth  trades    break-even  median if PERFECT
  ----------------------------------------------------
  weakest         881         57.7%             +83.3%
  weak            895         61.8%             +69.3%
  ok              771         63.2%             +58.0%
  strongest       841         38.5%             +85.3%

  Not monotone -- the middle two buckets are WORSE than the weakest, so
  this is not a dose-response. Only the top quartile stands out, and it
  does not hold up:

    top quartile     841 trades over  43 days   median  +22.5%   day-t +1.84
    the rest       2,547 trades over 252 days   median  -23.8%   day-t +2.61

    split in half by date, top-quartile edge over the rest:
      first half   +94.3pp
      second half  +20.6pp

  43 days, a day-t BELOW the bucket it is supposed to beat, and an edge
  that decays from +94pp to +21pp across the sample. There is no
  in-advance regime filter here. The regime risk has to be borne, not
  timed -- which is the real reason this is a market bet.
