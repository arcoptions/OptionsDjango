IS THE RETURN GAP ITSELF SIGNIFICANT?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  live wing  n 3,152  mean +38.2%  median -18.3%  t(day) +2.04  t(5d) +0.21

  no wing    n 236  mean +194.4%  median +100.5%  t(day) +5.36  t(5d) +2.02

  t(day) treats a session as one bet. t(5d) also spaces entries five
  sessions apart so no two trades share a holding period -- without it
  a five-day hold sampled daily reads about sqrt(5) too significant.
  Read the mean and the median together: a positive t beside a negative
  median is a lottery, not an edge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SANITY CHECK ON THE 'ALWAYS PAYS' BUCKET
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  wing          trades   premium   prem/spot   moneyness quotes/125    median
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  traded         3,152    28.30       3.45%       0.01%         38    -18.3%
  did not          236     4.97       1.83%      -0.09%         42   +100.5%

  All three fingerprints come back clean. The cheap bucket is 1.83% of
  spot against 3.45% -- a factor of 1.9, not the order of magnitude a
  broken print would show. Its moneyness is -0.09%, so it is genuinely
  at the money. And it carries MORE live quotes than the expensive
  bucket, 42 against 38, so the exit is not an intrinsic fallback.
  These are real, well-quoted, cheap contracts.

  Which is the answer, once you read premium as a clock rather than a
  price. An at-the-money call is worth roughly 3-4% of spot with a
  month to run and under 2% with days left, so a low prem/spot is not a
  bargain -- it is a contract close to expiry. And a contract close to
  expiry is exactly the one a five-session hold runs PAST, at which
  point the pinned strike silently becomes next month's option with a
  fresh month of time value. That is what pays when the stock falls.
  See expiry_contamination.py, which measures it: 3.7% break-even on
  the trades that cross, which is not an edge but an impossibility.

WHAT rungs_live ACTUALLY MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  rung            bars in cache      share
  ----------------------------------------
  ATM-1                  85,193       2.3%
  ATM                 1,173,347      31.7%
  ATM+1                 868,933      23.5%
  ATM+2               1,573,312      42.5%
  ATM+3                       0       0.0%

  The CALL cache holds ATM-1, ATM, ATM+1 and ATM+2; ATM+3 was never
  downloaded and ATM-1 landed only 85k bars against ATM's 1.17m. So a
  count of live rungs is substantially a count of which DOWNLOAD
  windows completed, which is not something a trader can see and not
  something the market decided. The 4-5 rung bucket in particular is
  mostly the 7% of dates where ATM-1 exists at all. That gradient
  cannot be traded until the cache is square, and it is not evidence
  of a liquidity effect while the download is still running.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The hypothesis was that a liquid chain lowers the hurdle. It does
  not: with the wing trading -- the liquid case -- the hurdle is 59.6%,
  WORSE than the 56.8% baseline. The apparent win sits entirely in the
  236 trades where the wing did NOT trade, and that bucket pays even
  when the stock falls, which no option does.

  So the 49.6% in the strike ladder's matched table is not a filter the
  user could run. It is what happens when you require three contracts
  to have printed, on a cache where printing is partly a function of
  what has finished downloading.

  The bucket that pays when the stock falls turned out to be worth more
  than the question that found it. Chasing why a cheap contract could
  do that led to the pricing bug behind it, and that bug is in the
  shipped 56.8% too -- see expiry_contamination.py. This file's own
  numbers carry it: read the 59.6% and the 56.8% as contaminated alike,
  which leaves the comparison between them intact and both levels wrong.
