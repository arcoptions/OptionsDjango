IS THE RETURN GAP ITSELF SIGNIFICANT?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  live wing  n 3,152  mean +26.1%  median -32.5%  t(day) -1.89  t(5d) -2.23

  no wing    n 236  mean +335.0%  median -23.9%  t(day) +1.30  t(5d) +0.99

  t(day) treats a session as one bet. t(5d) also spaces entries five
  sessions apart so no two trades share a holding period -- without it
  a five-day hold sampled daily reads about sqrt(5) too significant.
  Read the mean and the median together: a positive t beside a negative
  median is a lottery, not an edge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SANITY CHECK ON THE BUCKET THAT ONCE 'ALWAYS PAID'
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  wing          trades   premium   prem/spot   moneyness quotes/125    median
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  traded         3,152    28.30       3.45%       0.01%         38    -32.5%
  did not          236     4.97       1.83%      -0.09%         42    -23.9%

  All three fingerprints come back clean. The cheap bucket is 1.83% of
  spot against 3.45% -- a factor of 1.9, not the order of magnitude a
  broken print would show. Its moneyness is -0.09%, so it is genuinely
  at the money. And it carries 42 live quotes against 38, so the
  exit is not an intrinsic fallback. These are real, well-quoted,
  cheap contracts.

  Which was the answer, once you read premium as a clock rather
  than a price. An at-the-money call is worth roughly 3-4% of spot
  with a month to run and under 2% with days left, so a low
  prem/spot is not a bargain -- it is a contract close to expiry.
  And a contract close to expiry is exactly the one a five-session
  hold runs PAST, at which point the pinned strike silently becomes
  next month's option carrying a fresh month of time value. THAT is
  what used to pay when the stock fell. See expiry_contamination.py.

  The guard is now on, and the signature it was diagnosing is gone:
  this bucket returns -85.0% at the median when the stock does NOT
  rise, which is what a call is supposed to do. An impossibility
  that disappears when you fix the thing you blamed for it is
  the strongest confirmation this project has of a diagnosis.

WHAT rungs_live ACTUALLY MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  rung            bars in cache      share
  ----------------------------------------
  ATM-1                  85,193       2.3%
  ATM                 1,173,347      31.7%
  ATM+1                 868,933      23.5%
  ATM+2               1,573,312      42.5%
  ATM+3                       0       0.0%

  The CALL cache holds ATM-1, ATM, ATM+1 and ATM+2; ATM+3 was never
  downloaded and ATM-1 landed only 85k bars against ATM's 1.17m. So a
  count of live rungs is substantially a count of which DOWNLOAD
  windows completed, which is not something a trader can see and not
  something the market decided. The 4-5 rung bucket in particular is
  mostly the 7% of dates where ATM-1 exists at all. That gradient
  cannot be traded until the cache is square, and it is not evidence
  of a liquidity effect while the download is still running.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The hypothesis was that a liquid chain lowers the hurdle. It does
  not: with the wing trading -- the liquid case -- the hurdle is
  69.5%, WORSE than the 68.9% baseline. What is left sits entirely
  in the 236 trades where the wing did NOT trade.

  And that residue does NOT survive as a filter. It is 236 trades
  needing 60.3%, on a median of -23.9% with t(5d) +0.99. A
  positive t beside a negative median is a lottery, not an edge --
  and it can be scored in only 1 of its 15 months, because the
  bucket is too thin to read anywhere else.

  So the strike ladder's matched table is not a filter the user could
  run. It is what happens when you require three contracts to have
  printed, on a cache where printing is partly a function of what has
  finished downloading.

  THE PREDICTION THIS FILE MADE HAS NOW BEEN TESTED. It said the two
  hurdles were contaminated alike, so the COMPARISON would survive the
  expiry fix and the LEVELS would not. Re-run under the guard:
    liquid wing    69.5%   (was 59.6% unguarded)
    baseline       68.9%   (was 56.8% unguarded)
    no wing        60.3%   (was 49.6% unguarded)
  Both moved up about ten points and the liquid-minus-illiquid gap is
  9.2 points against the 10.0 it was before. The comparison held;
  the levels did not, exactly as predicted. Every number on this page
  is now the guarded one.
