{# ---- 4. the rule -------------------------------------------------------- #}
What the rule is, and why it is not a breakout

Every evening the F&O universe (20-day turnover of ₹{{ rule.turnover }} cr or more) is ranked on its 3, 6 and 12-month return. A leader is in the top {{ rule.leader }}%. A signal is a leader whose 2-day RSI closed below {{ rule.rsi }}: a strong stock having a sharp, short pullback. Buy at the next open, hold {{ rule.hold }} sessions, and exit early on a close {{ rule.stop }}% below the fill. {{ rule.slots }} equal slots; the strongest leaders are taken first.

Ten years of F&O data found no breakout — 52-week high, all-time high, Donchian, multi-touch horizontal level, gap-and-go — that beats the F&O average over 10 or 20 sessions, and buying a leader on its breakout day did worse than buying it on an ordinary day. Strength is what predicts; the dip is the better moment to act on it.
