{# ---- 4. why this exit plan ---------------------------------------------- #}
Why there is no stop-loss, and why ten sessions

Eight ways of cutting losses early and riding winners were tested on 6,600 signals from 2016 to 2026. Every early exit made it worse — a lower average trade and a deeper drawdown for the book. These are dip-buys: the losers usually recover within days, so a stop turns a temporary dip into a realised −50%, and the winners are the whole return, so a trail or target cuts off what pays for everything. What does control risk is time and size: sell on session {{ plan.hold }} (half the stock book's hold, before time decay bites) and keep one trade's premium near {{ risk_pct|floatformat:0 }}% of capital.
